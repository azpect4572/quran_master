Help

A quick guide to using Quran Master.

Getting Started

From the home page, click Surah Search to find the Surah you want to recite. You can search for it either by typing its name (for example "Al-Fatiha") or its number (for example "1") into the search box.

Click on any Surah's card to open it. This takes you straight to the first Ayah of that Surah, ready for you to start listening and reciting.

The Recitation Screen

Once you've opened a Surah, four buttons are available for each Ayah:

  • Play - listen to the reciter reading this Ayah.
  • Record - start recording your own voice reciting the Ayah.
  • Stop - stops whatever is currently happening, whether that's the reciter's audio playing or your own recording.
  • Submit Recitation - sends your recording off to be scored, once you're happy with it.

The usual order to use them in is:

  1. Press Play to listen to the Ayah first.
  2. Press Record and recite the Ayah yourself.
  3. Press Stop when you've finished reciting.
  4. Press Submit Recitation to see your score.

Changing the Reciter

At the top of the recitation screen there's a Change Reciter dropdown menu. This lets you pick which reciter's voice you want to listen to and learn from.

Whoever you pick is used two ways: it's the recitation you hear when you press Play, and it's also who your own recitation is compared against when it's scored. Changing reciters will change both.

Play Full Surah

If you want to listen through a whole Surah without pressing Play for every single Ayah, click the small settings cog (⚙) icon near the top of the recitation screen, then choose Play Full Surah.

This plays every Ayah in the Surah in order automatically, moving on to the next one by itself once each Ayah finishes, so you can just sit back and listen.

How Scoring Works

When you press Submit Recitation, your recording is compared against the reference reciter's audio in three ways: how long it takes to say (duration), how much you pause while reciting (pauses), and how loud your voice is (volume). These three comparisons are combined into a single score out of 100.

Your score falls into one of three rating bands:

Good Score of 80-100. Great recitation, very close to the reference.
Satisfactory Score of 41-79. A reasonable attempt, with room to improve.
Weak Score of 0-40. Worth practicing this Ayah again.

The Progress Tracker

The Progress Tracker page shows four summary numbers at the top:

  • Surahs Completed - how many Surahs you've fully finished with a good score.
  • Average Score - your average score across every Surah you've started.
  • Over 80% - how many of your started Surahs are scoring well.
  • Under 40% - how many of your started Surahs still need more practice.

Each Surah in the list also shows a status:

Completed You've attempted every Ayah and your score is high enough.
In Progress You've started this Surah, but haven't finished it yet.
Not Started You haven't attempted any Ayah in this Surah yet.

Clicking on a Surah shows its Ayah-by-Ayah breakdown, along with a Needs Practice section underneath - this lists the lowest-scoring Ayahs you've already attempted in that Surah, so you know exactly what to practice next.

Troubleshooting

My microphone isn't working. The first time you press Record, your browser will ask for permission to use your microphone - make sure you click "Allow". If you accidentally blocked it before, you'll need to open your browser's site settings for this page and allow the microphone there instead.

Submit Recitation won't work. Your recording needs to actually contain your voice before it can be submitted. If you press Record and then Stop straight away without saying anything, there's nothing to send - make sure you recite the Ayah before pressing Stop.
